The Martini is having a moment. The timeless classic never went away, of course, but now it’s everywhere, and is as customisable as ever – wet or dry, olive or twist. But the primary decision, invariably, is between gin and vodka, and, depending on who you speak to, the latter is becoming increasingly common.

Whether this is linked with changing consumer preferences regarding gin, or fuelled by the rise of the often vodka-based Dirty Martini, or any number of other influences, there’s little doubt that the Martini’s current ubiquity is proving to be a boon to the vodka category.
