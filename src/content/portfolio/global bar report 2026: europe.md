Despite ongoing challenges – inflation, cost of living, rising operating costs, you name it – there’s plenty to be optimistic about in Europe’s bar scene. Cocktail consumption is up in some countries, operators are adapting to demand for more moderation-minded options, and the scene is no longer centred around just a few established cities.

“One of the most exciting things about the European bar scene today is how diverse and connected it has become,” says Nicolas Goradesky, bar manager at The Cambridge Public House in Paris. “Every country and city has its own identity, but there’s a real openness to sharing ideas, techniques and experiences.”
