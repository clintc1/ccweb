Created by four university friends in partnership with a maestro mezcalero in Mexico’s Michoacán, Zacal offers an alternative to the many mezcals from Oaxaca, the majority of which are made from Espadín agave.

Plans for the brand began in 2020, during lockdown, when Phil Clayton, Andrew Maidment, Christian Osborne and Ian McCaig decided the time had come to create a spirit brand together and settled on mezcal. After a few years of research, visits to Mexico to find the right partner, product development and inevitable shipping delays, Zacal finally launched in the UK in 2025.
