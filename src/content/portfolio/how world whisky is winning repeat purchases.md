The world of whisky is broader than it’s ever been. Established producing countries continue to dominate – in admittedly difficult times – but challengers have been emerging over the past few years from all over the globe, bringing new approaches, new climates and new stories into the picture. It’s still early days for many of these though, and what’s novel and exciting in the industry has yet to reach most consumers.

This broader international spread within the category is starting to be evident in the selection available from retailers such as The Whisky Exchange in the UK. “Over the past year, we’ve done a lot with Korean whisky and are continuing to explore new producers as they come online,” says commercial buying director Dawn Davies MW.
